Network sharing from 0.6.0 is withdrawn while it is reworked.

- The **Share sites on this network** switch, the LAN listener, its port and its mDNS names are gone, along with `paddock lan` and the matching Doctor checks.
- If sharing was on in 0.6.0, nothing answers on the network once this update is installed. Sites, services and databases are back to their usual loopback-only access.
- Everything else works as it did in 0.5.5.
