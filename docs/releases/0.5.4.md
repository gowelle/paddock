Approve a site's tasks once, and they keep running across reboots.

- After a restart of your Mac, Paddock read every approved site as a folder that had been replaced: its tasks (queue worker, Reverb, scheduler) stopped, and forwarding paths such as `/app` were dropped, which broke WebSockets for Laravel Reverb.
- Paddock now tells a site's folder apart by its inode and creation time, which last, instead of the volume's device number, which macOS reassigns at each reboot. Existing approvals hold again without approving them a second time.
