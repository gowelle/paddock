Share chosen sites with phones, tablets and other computers on your network.

- **Settings › Sites** gains a **Share sites on this network** switch and a tick beside each site. Off by default; only the sites you tick answer on the network.
- Shared sites open at `http://<site>.paddock.local:8080` (or the next free port) over plain HTTP, announced with mDNS. A device that can't resolve those names can open the numeric address shown in the app; it reaches a landing page listing what is shared.
- **Copy Network Link** in the menu bar, and a QR button beside each shared site, put a site's address on another device.
- The command line follows: `paddock lan on`, `lan off`, `lan share <site>`, `lan unshare <site>`, and `paddock status` shows the address. Doctor gained checks for the listener, this Mac's address and the port.
- The LAN listener's log appears in the Logs window. If it stops on its own, Paddock withdraws the names and `paddock start` brings it back.
- Nothing else is reachable there: services, Mailpit, databases, dumps and the control socket stay loopback-only, and nothing new runs as root. There is no login, so share only on a network you trust.
