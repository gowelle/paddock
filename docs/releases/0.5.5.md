Paddock tells you when it has given up restarting something, updates services from Settings, and reads logs that aren't Laravel's.

- When a task or service crashes five times in a minute, Paddock stops restarting it and says so: a notification with **Restart** and **Open Log**, and a red dot on the menu bar gate until it recovers. Each problem is announced at most once every ten minutes.
- A service with a newer version shows **Update to 18.7.0** beside Start and Stop, updates it in place, and says what now runs when it is done.
- The Logs window marks levels in PHP-FPM, PostgreSQL, MySQL/MariaDB and Caddy's JSON logs, so their errors fold and their context reads like the rest; **Errors only** narrows any log to errors and above.
